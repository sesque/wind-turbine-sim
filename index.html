What the turbine makes

Power

Counting 1 kW per home.

Rotor speed
rpm

rpm = rotations per minute.

Blade tip speed
km/h

How fast the tip of a blade moves.

Blade pitch
°

How far each blade is twisted to spill extra wind.

Overall efficiency

The share of the wind's power that reaches the grid.

The formula

P = ½ × ρ × A × v³

P is the power in the wind. ρ (say "rho") is the weight of air, fixed at 1.2 kg/m³. A is the circle the blades sweep, in m². v is the wind speed.
