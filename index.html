      Where the energy goes

      Wind energy goes in on one side. A little is lost at each stage, mostly as heat. What is left reaches the grid (the power network that carries electricity to homes).

      The formula

      This is the rule for the power in the wind. The wind speed is cubed (multiplied by itself three times), so wind speed matters most.

      P = ½ × ρ × A × v³

      P is the power in the wind. ρ (say "rho") is the weight of air, fixed at 1.2 kg/m³. A is the circle the blades sweep, in m². v is the wind speed.
